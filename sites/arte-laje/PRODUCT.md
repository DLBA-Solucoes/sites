## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static), Tailwind CSS 4, vanilla JS islands. Part of the `sites` monorepo (`sites/arte-laje`). GitHub Pages preview at `/sites/arte-laje/`.

## Users

Primary: people building in the Vale do Itapocu region (Jaraguá do Sul and around): homeowners building a house, builders and mestres de obra, small contractors, engineers and architects specifying slabs. They arrive with a project in hand and want a quote, a recommendation of slab type, and delivery on the planned date.

## Product Purpose

Present Arte Laje (Jaraguá do Sul, since 1977) as the regional precast slab factory it is, explain the slab types simply, and make the next step obvious: send the project for a quote or call a sales representative. Success: a visitor sends a project or calls.

Also a sales demo: shown to Arte Laje side by side with their current site, built with their real content.

## Positioning

Pioneer in precast slabs in the Vale do Itapocu (since 1977), PBQP-H member since 2004, own factory in Nova Brasília, civil engineer on staff with ART for every slab, assembly scheme delivered with each order, financing directly with the company. Slogan: "Nosso negócio é concreto".

## Product Principles

1. Show how a slab is made and assembled: diagrams and real factory photos over generic construction stock.
2. One clear action: send the project (or call).
3. Plain words for the homeowner, precise facts for the engineer.
4. Contact names and numbers always reachable on the phone.
